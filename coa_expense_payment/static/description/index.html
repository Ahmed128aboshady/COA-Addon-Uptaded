How It Works

Three Steps from Expense to Posted Entry

The entire expense voucher lifecycle — from creation to accounting — handled in a structured, repeatable workflow.

📝
STEP 01

Create Voucher

Open the Expense Payments menu and click New. Fill in the payee, payment date, journal, and expense category. Add as many expense lines as required, selecting the appropriate account and tax for each line. The system auto-assigns a sequential voucher number.

New Record Add Lines Auto Reference
🧮
STEP 02

Review & Tax Calculation

As you enter amounts, the system instantly recomputes all tax values and updates the voucher totals. Review the tax summary panel, verify line totals, and confirm the payable amount. Click Confirm to lock the voucher and move it to the Confirmed state for manager review.

Auto Tax Total Review Confirm
✅
STEP 03

Post & Print

Click Post to generate the journal entry automatically in Odoo Accounting. The voucher status advances to Posted. Use the Print Voucher button to download the professionally formatted PDF, ready for filing, payment authorization, or external submission.

Post Entry Print PDF Archive

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from finance teams and implementation partners.

Q

Does this module replace Odoo's built-in Expense (hr_expense) module?

A

No — it complements it. The COA Expense Payment Voucher module focuses on structured payment voucher creation and accounting integration for company-level expenses (legal, licensing, hospitality, etc.). It depends on hr_expense for expense categories but adds its own dedicated voucher model, workflow states, and printable PDF output on top of the native Odoo expense infrastructure.

Q

Can I configure which journal is used for posting expense payment vouchers?

A

Yes. Each voucher includes a Journal field that lets you select any configured journal in your Odoo instance — whether that is a bank journal, cash journal, or a dedicated expense journal. This gives your accounting team full control over how expense entries are recorded and which accounts they flow through in your chart of accounts.

Q

Is the generated journal entry reversible if a voucher was posted in error?

A

Yes. Because the module creates a standard Odoo account.move upon posting, you benefit from all of Odoo Accounting's native capabilities — including the Reset to Draft and Reversal actions available on any posted journal entry. Simply navigate to the linked journal entry from the voucher smart button and use the standard reversal workflow.
