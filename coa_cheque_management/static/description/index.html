Core Capabilities

Designed for Professional Accounting Teams

📥 Incoming Cheques (Customer PDCs)

Record customer cheques against sales invoices. Track drawer, bank, cheque leaf number, and maturity date with automatic Notes Receivable lines.

📤 Outgoing Cheques (Vendor Payments)

Issue cheques to suppliers and subcontractors. Manage checkbook leaves, link purchase bills, and debit bank accounts precisely upon clearance.

⚡ Automated Double-Entry Journaling

System generates journal entries automatically for Notes Receivable, Notes Payable, Cheques Under Collection, and Bank accounts at each stage.

↩️ 1-Click Bounce & Reversal Workflow

Handle returned cheques (NSF) seamlessly. The module automatically reverses posted entries, updates the cheque status, and restores customer balance.

🏢 Multi-Company & Analytic Linkage

Multi-branch and multi-company ready. Distribute cheque values across analytic cost centers and contracting projects for granular cash tracking.

🔒 Security Groups & Audit Trail

Segregation of duties between cashiers, accountants, and financial controllers. Every status change is logged in Odoo chatter with full audit trail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this module modify standard Odoo accounting tables?

No. It creates dedicated models and uses standard Odoo journal items, guaranteeing 100% database integrity and zero migration conflicts.

Can we configure custom intermediate accounts?

Yes! From Accounting Settings, you can select custom balance sheet accounts for Notes Receivable, Notes Payable, and Cheques Under Collection.
