Core Capabilities

Enterprise Financial Features

📥 Incoming Cheques (Customer PDCs)

Accept cheques against sales invoices or customer advances. Record drawer, bank branch, cheque leaf number, and maturity date with automatic Notes Receivable lines.

📤 Outgoing Cheques (Vendor Payments)

Issue cheques to suppliers, subcontractors, and service providers. Track issued leaves, link purchase bills, and debit your bank account upon maturity.

⚡ Automated Double-Entry Journaling

Eliminate human accounting error. The system posts proper entries to Cheques Under Collection, Notes Receivable, Notes Payable, and Bank Accounts automatically.

↩️ 1-Click Bounce & Reversal Workflow

Handle returned cheques (NSF) seamlessly. The system auto-reverses posted entries, flags the cheque as cancelled/bounced, and restores customer or vendor debt.

🏢 Multi-Company & Analytic Linkage

Multi-branch and multi-company friendly. Tag cheques with analytic distribution accounts (Cost Centers / Projects) for complete cash-flow visibility.

🔒 Security Groups & Audit Trail

Clear segregation of duties between cashiers, senior accountants, and treasury controllers. Every status transition is logged in Odoo chatter with full audit trail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this module modify standard Odoo accounting tables?

No. It creates dedicated models for incoming and outgoing cheques and links to standard journal items without overriding core tables, guaranteeing 100% database integrity and easy future version upgrades.

Can we configure custom intermediate accounts for cheques?

Yes! Under Accounting ➔ Configuration ➔ Settings, you can configure the specific balance sheet accounts for Notes Receivable, Notes Payable, and Cheques Under Collection per company.

Is this module compatible with Odoo Enterprise?

Yes! It works identically on both Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise across versions 17.0, 18.0, and 19.0.
