Why Your Business Needs This App

Managing cheques manually on spreadsheets or using generic payment journals leads to reconciliation errors, untracked post-dated cheques, and cash-flow blind spots.

✕

Without COA Cheque Module

  • ✕ Unmonitored PDCs: Post-dated cheques get lost in drawers without maturity notifications.
  • ✕ Manual Journal Entries: Finance staff must draft complex debits and credits manually at each stage.

Comprehensive Capabilities

Designed for Demanding Accounting Teams

Everything you need to control financial papers, streamline Treasury operations, and maintain audit compliance inside Odoo.

📥

Incoming Cheques (Customer PDCs)

Accept cheques from clients against sales invoices or down-payments. Record cheque drawer, bank branch, cheque number, and due date with automated Notes Receivable entries.

📤

Outgoing Cheques (Vendor Payments)

Issue cheques to suppliers, contractors, and service providers. Track issued leaves, link with purchase bills, and debit your bank account precisely upon maturity and clearing.

⚡

Automated Double-Entry Accounting

Eliminate human accounting error. The system posts appropriate entries to Cheques Under Collection, Notes Receivable, Notes Payable, and Bank Accounts at each lifecycle phase.

↩️

Bounced & Rejection Workflow

Handle NSF (Non-Sufficient Funds) or returned cheques seamlessly. The system reverses previously posted entries, flags the cheque, and restores partner debt in one click.

🏢

Multi-Company & Analytic Linkage

Multi-branch and multi-company friendly. Tag cheques with Analytic Distribution accounts (Cost Centers / Projects) to monitor project cash outflows and contract collections.

🔒

Role-Based Security & Audit Trail

Segregation of duties between cashiers, senior accountants, and financial controllers. Full audit trail recorded in Odoo chatter with timestamps and responsible users.

Visual Workflow

Cheque Journey from Receipt to Bank Clearance

Every stage automatically generates the corresponding financial journal entry according to professional Egyptian and International Accounting Standards.

Step 01

Draft Registration

Treasurer creates the cheque leaf: payee/issuer, bank, cheque number, amount, maturity date, and linked partner.

Accounting: None (Draft status)
Step 02

Confirmation & Hold

Cheque is approved by accounting. The leaf is safely kept in the company safe pending maturity or bank deposit.

Dr: Notes Receivable
Cr: Customer Account
Step 03

Bank Deposit

Upon maturity, the cheque is sent to the clearing bank. Status transitions to "Under Collection".

Dr: Cheques Under Collection
Cr: Notes Receivable
Step 04

Clearance / Settlement

Bank statement confirms actual fund clearance. Cheque is marked as Done and reconciled with bank journal.

Dr: Main Bank Account
Cr: Cheques Under Collection

Need Clarification?

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this module alter core Odoo accounting tables?

No. The module follows Odoo standard architectural best practices. It creates clean dedicated models (`incoming.cheque` and `outgoing.cheque`) and interacts with standard `account.move` and `account.move.line` records without modifying base tables or breaking upgrades.

Can we configure custom intermediate accounts for cheques?

Yes! Through Accounting → Configuration → Settings, you can select which specific balance sheet accounts represent Notes Receivable, Notes Payable, and Cheques Under Collection for each company.

Is this module compatible with Odoo Enterprise?

Yes, it works identically on both Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise Editions across versions 17.0, 18.0, and 19.0.
