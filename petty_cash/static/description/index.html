Core Capabilities

Six Feature Pillars

Every element of petty cash management covered end-to-end, from issuance through to ledger posting.

🗂️

Petty Cash Custodies

Create and manage multiple open custodies simultaneously — one per employee, department, or project. Each custody tracks its holder, issued amount, remaining balance, and lifecycle status from Draft through to Settled.

  • →Named custodian with employee link
  • →Approved fund amount & currency
  • →Status: Draft → Confirmed → In Progress → Settled
  • →Linked expenses aggregated automatically
🏷️

Expense Categories & Classification

Define a hierarchy of expense categories that map directly to your chart of accounts. Custodians select the correct category when entering each expense, ensuring correct account coding without accountant intervention.

  • →Configurable category master list
  • →Category-to-account mapping
  • →Vendor name & narration per expense
  • →Expense date & amount tracking
✅

Multi-Level Approval Workflow

Settlement requests pass through a structured approval chain before accounting entries are posted. Each level receives automated notification. Rejections return the request to the custodian with reviewer comments for correction.

  • →Configurable approver levels
  • →Email & in-app notifications
  • →Approve / Reject with comments
  • →Full chatter log on every action
⚖️

Settlement & Reconciliation

At settlement, the system automatically computes total expenses versus the issued fund, shows the remaining balance or shortfall, and generates a return payment or reimbursement request. Custody is then marked Settled and locked.

  • →Automatic balance calculation
  • →Over/under-spend handling
  • →Custody lock after settlement
  • →Printable settlement report
📒

Automated Journal Entries

On fund issuance and on final settlement, Odoo automatically creates and posts the correct accounting journal entries to the petty cash float account, expense accounts, and cash or bank. No manual bookkeeping required.

  • →Issuance entry: Cash → Petty Cash Float
  • →Settlement: Expense Accounts Dr, Float Cr
  • →Configurable journal & accounts
  • →Direct link to Odoo accounting moves
🔐

Role-Based Access & Audit Trail

Separate security groups for Custodians, Managers, and Finance ensure that users can only see and act on records within their authority. Every state change, approval, and edit is logged in the chatter for full auditability.

  • →Custodian / Manager / Finance roles
  • →Record-level access rules
  • →Chatter log on every custody & expense
  • →Immutable settled records
End-to-End Process

4-Step Workflow Pipeline

A clear, enforced process that every petty cash request follows — from fund issuance to final ledger entry.

💼
STEP 01

Create Custody & Fund Transfer

Finance creates a custody record, assigns a custodian, sets the fund amount, and posts the issuance journal entry.

🧾
STEP 02

Record Expenses with Receipts

Custodian adds individual expense lines with date, vendor, category, amount, and attaches scanned receipts.

🔏
STEP 03

Submit for Approval

Custodian submits the settlement request. Approvers are notified and review each expense before approving or returning.

✅
STEP 04

Settlement & Journal Posting

Finance confirms settlement; Odoo auto-posts the expense journal entries. Custody is reconciled and locked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from finance teams before going live.

Q

Can one employee hold multiple open custodies at the same time?

A

Yes. The module places no restriction on the number of simultaneous open custodies per employee or department. Each custody is independently tracked with its own fund limit, expense list, and settlement. This is particularly useful for project-based organizations where an employee may manage separate budgets for different projects concurrently.

Q

What happens to the accounting entries when a custody is settled?

A

Upon final settlement approval, the module automatically generates and posts a journal entry that debits the relevant expense accounts (based on each expense line's category-to-account mapping) and credits the petty cash float account. If there is a remaining balance returned by the custodian, an additional entry credits cash/bank and debits the float. All moves are directly linked to the custody record and appear in the standard Odoo accounting journal for full transparency.

Q

Do we need any additional Odoo modules installed to use COA Petty Cash?

A

The module depends only on three core Odoo modules: base, account (Accounting), and hr (Human Resources). Both account and hr are standard modules included in every Odoo instance by default, so no additional third-party modules are required. Simply install petty_cash from the Apps menu and the system is ready to use.
