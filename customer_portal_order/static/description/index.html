How the Flow Works

A completely automated cycle from portal request submission to internal sales order approval.

1

Portal Request

The customer logs in, searches products, fills quantities, selects billing/shipping addresses, and submits.

2

Back-Office Sync

Odoo automatically creates a draft Quotation (Sale Order), calculates pricing, and links customer accounts.

3

Notification & State

The quotation moves to "Quotation Sent", sending notifications to internal staff and providing immediate receipt.

4

Review & Confirm

Your sales representative reviews the draft order, applies discounts or adjusts details, and confirms the sale.

Designed for Modern B2B and Wholesale

Traditional B2B customer ordering via email, chat, or phone calls is slow, error-prone, and manually taxing. Our module solves this by giving your customers direct portal autonomy.

  • Reduced Admin Overhead: Cut manual sales data entry in half.
  • No More Mismatched Data: Autocomplete matches direct Odoo database product IDs.
  • Instant Portal Confirmation: Customers get direct order receipt reference codes.
  • Multiple Client Branches: Dynamically handles child contacts and custom shipping branches.
  • No Hidden Modifications: Clean Odoo architecture, uses standard sale.order and portal objects.
