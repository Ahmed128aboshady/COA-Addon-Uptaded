Business Solution Overview

Direct B2B portal ordering system integrated with Odoo Sales backend.

The Business Challenge

Manual processing of customer orders received via phone, email, or messaging channels increases administration overhead and introduces transactional errors. Customers lack real-time visibility into product catalogs, prices, and branches directly from their dashboard, forcing sales teams to manually enter drafts and verify information.

Our Solution

This module provides a secure, self-service order request dashboard within the standard Odoo Customer Portal. Customers search the active catalog using live databases, select shipping and billing addresses linked to their commercial contacts, add delivery notes, and submit. The system instantly generates a draft sales quotation in the Odoo backend, reducing administrative delays and eliminating order discrepancies.

Key Capabilities

Functional details of the portal ordering integration.

Real-Time Autocomplete Search

Features an interactive product search bar querying live catalogs. Matches by item name or barcode to prevent incorrect product selection.

Branch Address Allocation

Supports multi-branch accounts. Customers can choose custom invoicing and shipping destinations linked to their profile.

Instant Quotation Generation

Creates draft sales orders immediately in Odoo ERP upon portal submission. Automatically calculates initial catalog pricing.

Order Processing Workflow

System tracking from portal submission to warehouse fulfillment.

1
Portal Entry & Validation

The client accesses their portal, checks real-time product lines, inputs billing/shipping addresses, and submits the order request.

2
Automated Quotation Generation

The Odoo backend automatically generates a draft Quotation (Sale Order) pre-populating client profiles, selected items, and pricing structures.

3
Internal Status Update & Review

The quotation moves to the 'Quotation Sent' status in the backend. Internal sales representatives receive alerts to review catalog lines and discounts.

4
Sales Confirmation & Processing

Upon review, the manager approves the quotation, converting it to a confirmed Sale Order and triggering downstream warehouse pickings.
