Customer Portal: Order Request

by Community of Accountants

Enable B2B clients and wholesale buyers to submit order requests directly from their native Odoo Customer Portal dashboard. Eliminate manual emails, coordinate delays, and typo errors.

Customers check live product listings by name or reference code, review pre-assigned pricing, select billing/delivery branch addresses, and submit. The system instantly compiles a backend draft sale quotation.

Seamless B2B Self-Service Ordering Interface

Streamline transaction processing for repeat wholesale customers. Clients can search, adjust, and request orders 24/7 without needing direct administrative access to your Odoo ERP.

Why Choose Customer Portal Order

A clean, high-performance, and secure ordering loop designed for commercial business relationships.

Direct Autocomplete Sync

Live search queries products directly from your inventory database. Prevents catalog mismatches or invalid code submissions.

Zero Performance Impact

Controller endpoints are light and run at the search index layer, ensuring instant response times even with extensive catalog databases.

Address & Branch Allocation

Retrieves all registered delivery and billing addresses associated with the client's commercial card. Excellent for multi-branch customers.

Native Sales Integration

Integrates natively with the Odoo Sales application. Generates draft quotations, moving them to 'Quotation Sent' to trigger standard flows.

All-in-One Portal Ordering Capabilities

Engineered to automate your sales ingestion cycle and reduce admin touchpoints.

Live Product Autocomplete

Search products dynamically by name, barcode, or internal reference with instant availability status.

Customer Pricelist Alignment

Fetches and displays B2B rates directly based on the logged-in client partner pricelist configurations.

Multi-Address Routing

Allows buyers to separate billing and shipping destinations associated with their business profile.

Automated Backend Creation

Instantly compiles and stores a draft quotation, avoiding human data-entry or copy-paste overhead.

Quotation Sent Notifications

Pushes sale state to 'Quotation Sent', triggering standard Odoo mail integrations to log history and send confirmation receipts.

Mobile Responsive Form

Composed with clean Bootstrap CSS compatibility. Looks and works perfectly on cell phones, tablets, and computers.
