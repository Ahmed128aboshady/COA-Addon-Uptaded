Sales Portal Integration

Self-Service Portal Ordering

Align client portal interactions with standard sales workflows to reduce operational delays.

Key Deliverables

Manual data entry of customer purchase requests from chats or emails consumes valuable team resources. This module creates a direct interface in the Odoo Customer Portal, allowing registered partners to execute order requests autonomously.

  • Live product catalog verification by barcode or name.
  • Automated pricing retrieval from active Odoo pricelists.
  • Dynamic selection of commercial shipping and billing branches.
  • Direct back-office draft quotation generation.

Core Positioning

Features outline the mechanics, but process clarity ensures daily transactional stability.

  • Reduces order receipt errors.
  • Secures data integrity.
  • Maintains Odoo CRM workflow.

1 Live Autocomplete Search

Enables product queries directly in the database. Matches item name or barcode to prevent incorrect line entries.

2 Multi-Branch Allocation

Retrieves shipping and invoicing contacts associated with the parent commercial record for localized deliveries.

3 Auto-Draft Quotation

Creates draft sales orders immediately. Sets status to 'Quotation Sent' to alert internal staff and log notifications.

Support & FAQ

Got Questions?

Detailed support structure and common configuration FAQs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the system calculate product prices in the portal?

A: It retrieves prices dynamically based on the active pricelist assigned to the logged-in customer's partner record.

Q: Does the customer get an email confirmation immediately?

A: Yes. Because Odoo creates a draft quotation and moves it to 'Quotation Sent', standard Odoo mail actions trigger and send notifications.

Q: Can I customize the layout of the portal form?

A: Yes, QWeb view templates are used. You can override `portal_order_form` template easily via standard Odoo inheritance.
