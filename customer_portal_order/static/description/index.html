B2B Self-Service — Without Touching Your ERP

Traditional order intake requires customers to email, call, or manually enter data into your system. This module gives wholesale buyers a professional ordering portal tied directly to their Odoo pricelist, addresses, and product catalog — generating a draft quotation automatically with a single click.

Core Features

Everything Your B2B Customers Need

A complete ordering loop — from product search to quotation creation — without leaving the portal.

Live Product Autocomplete

Customers search by product name or internal reference code. Results are fetched directly from your live inventory catalog in real time.

Pricelist-Aware Pricing

Displays each product's unit price from the partner's assigned B2B pricelist. Prevents unauthorized price lookups or discount mismatches.

Multi-Address Support

Fetches all billing and shipping addresses tied to the partner. Perfect for multi-branch B2B clients with different delivery destinations.

Draft Quotation Creation

Submission instantly creates a sale order in draft status. No data entry needed — your team receives a ready-to-review quotation.

Quotation Sent Notification

After creation, the order is set to "Quotation Sent" state — triggering standard Odoo email notifications and chatter logs automatically.

Mobile Responsive

The portal form is fully responsive. Your customers can place order requests from desktop, tablet, or smartphone with the same professional experience.

Workflow

How It Works — 4 Simple Steps

From customer login to backend quotation — fully automated, zero manual handling.

1
Customer Logs into Their Portal

The client accesses the native Odoo Customer Portal with their existing credentials. A new "Order Request" menu item appears automatically — no configuration required on the customer side.

2
Selects Addresses & Searches Products

The system pre-loads all billing and delivery addresses for the partner. The customer searches products live from your catalog, seeing their specific B2B pricing per item.

3
Builds and Submits the Order

Products are added to the order list with adjustable quantities. Once satisfied, the customer clicks Submit — triggering an instant backend call to create the sale order.

4
Your Team Receives a Ready-to-Confirm Quotation

A draft sale quotation appears in the Odoo backend with all line items, addresses, and partner data populated. The status is set to "Quotation Sent" — Odoo email notifications fire automatically.

Full Capabilities

Everything Built Into the Module

No third-party services, no external APIs — entirely native Odoo architecture.

Product Autocomplete Engine

Real-time product lookup by name, reference, or barcode. Returns matching results with unit pricing from partner pricelist.

Pricelist Price Rendering

Prices are resolved from the partner's assigned pricelist. Customers only see their negotiated B2B rates, never the public price.

Partner Address Loader

Retrieves all commercial, delivery, and invoice addresses from the partner's res.partner tree. Displayed in clear dropdown selectors.

Instant Sale.Order Creation

Python controller writes a complete sale order with all lines, addresses, and partner data on submit. No queue or delay involved.

Quotation Sent State Trigger

After creation, the quotation state advances to "Quotation Sent" — firing Odoo standard mail templates and chatter log entries.

Mobile-Optimized Portal Form

Bootstrap-compatible, responsive QWeb template. Renders cleanly across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers without extra CSS overhead.
