In-Depth Key Features & Technical Capabilities

1. BOQ Estimation & Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

  • Creates multi-level Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with itemized cost pricing, estimated quantities, and profit margins.
  • Integrates directly with Odoo Project tasks, milestones, and GANTT schedules.
  • Supports structural, architectural, MEP, and finishing work classifications.

2. Subcontractor Contracts & Retention Management

  • Manages subcontractor tenders, agreements, advance payments, and work completion certificates.
  • Automates Retention Money deductions (e.g. 5% or 10%) held until project warranty handover.
  • Prevents over-billing by checking cumulative subcontractor invoices against certified work progress.

3. Site Daily Work Logs & Equipment Allocation

  • Records site daily progress reports including workforce headcounts, weather delays, and site supervisor notes.
  • Tracks heavy machinery operating hours, fuel consumption, and equipment rental expenses per project.
  • Captures site photos and mobile digital sign-offs from resident engineers.

4. Real-Time Project Costing & Profitability Audit

  • Compares actual site expenses (materials, labor, equipment, subcontractors) against original BOQ budget in real-time.
  • Generates professional QWeb PDF Construction Progress Certificates and Client Invoices.
  • Fully compatible with Odoo 17.0, 18.0, and 19.0 Community & Enterprise Editions.

Q: Is this construction suite compatible with both Odoo Community and Enterprise?
Yes! COA Construction Management is 100% compatible with Odoo 17.0, 18.0, and 19.0 across both Community and Enterprise editions without requiring extra third-party modules.
Q: Does it support Subcontractor Retention Money deductions?
Yes. The suite allows setting retention percentages on subcontractor contracts and automatically holds retention amounts until project warranty certificate release.
