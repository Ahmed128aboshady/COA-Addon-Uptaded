COA Account Journal Commission Suite

Comprehensive enterprise-grade solution for Odoo developed by COA (Community of Accountants). Built for maximum reliability, speed, and seamless integration across all business workflows.

In-Depth Functional Capabilities & Key Features

1. Comprehensive Financial & Accounting Control

  • Streamlines general ledger entries, journal validations, and financial reconciliation.
  • Automates multi-currency journal adjustments, exchange gain/loss, and balance tracking.
  • Seamless integration with Odoo Invoicing, Accounting, and Bank Reconciliation.

2. Automated Payment & Cheque Management

  • Tracks PDC (Post Dated Cheques), letters of guarantee, and bank payment vouchers.
  • Provides clear visibility on vendor bills, customer advance deposits, and settlement status.
  • Includes automated notifications for due dates and bank clearing status.

3. Restrict Access & Audit Log Security

  • Restricts sensitive financial journals, ledger lines, and account balance modifications by user group.
  • Maintains a complete digital audit trail of all entry postings, reverses, and cancellations.
  • Ensures strict compliance with accounting controls and auditing standards.

4. Custom Financial Vouchers & PDF Reporting

  • Prints elegant Arabic & English PDF financial vouchers, partner statements, and GL summary sheets.
  • Optimized for fast generation with zero impact on database performance.
  • Backed by certified financial accounting experts at COA (Community of Accountants).
