In-Depth Key Features & Technical Capabilities

1. Mandatory Technical Review Gatekeeper

  • Enforces a mandatory technical validation stage before any sales quotation can be confirmed into a binding Sales Order.
  • Includes a specialized Rejection Wizard allowing engineers to send quotations back to sales reps with mandatory corrective notes.
  • Strict role-based access rights ensuring only designated Technical Office Managers can authorize BOQ changes.

2. Volumetric & Scrap Waste Ratio Calculator

  • Supports multi-dimensional measurements: Length, Width, Thickness, Surface Area (m²), and Cubic Volume (m³).
  • Configurable Scrap/Waste percentage (Waste Ratio %) automatically added to raw material requirements to prevent shortage.
  • Ideal for Marble, Granite, Woodworking, Glass, Aluminum, Fabrication, and Contracting industries.

3. Automated BOM & Manufacturing Order (MO) Generation

  • Automatically calculates raw material quantities and creates dynamic Bill of Materials (BOM) formulas based on customer specifications.
  • Instantly generates draft or confirmed Manufacturing Orders (MO) in Odoo MRP as soon as the technical review is approved.
  • Eliminates manual data re-entry and prevents human error between sales desk and production floor.

4. Technical QWeb PDF Reports & Audit Trail

  • Generates professional bilingual QWeb PDF Technical Review Sheets containing full BOQ breakdown, dimensions, and engineer signatures.
  • Maintains full audit log tracking of all technical modifications, price adjustments, and approval timestamps.
  • Fully compatible with Odoo 17.0, 18.0, and 19.0 Community & Enterprise Editions.
