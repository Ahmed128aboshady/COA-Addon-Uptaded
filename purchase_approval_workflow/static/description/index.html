Core Capabilities

Six Pillars of Procurement Governance

Each feature is engineered to close a specific gap in your purchase authorization framework.

💰

Amount-Based Approval Tiers

Define unlimited approval tiers with minimum and maximum monetary thresholds. When a PO is submitted, the system automatically identifies the applicable tier and routes to the correct approver group. Threshold currency is configurable per company.

  • Configurable min/max per tier
  • Multi-company & currency support
  • Sequential & parallel approval modes
🏷️

Vendor & Category Filters

Layer additional approval conditions on top of amount rules. Specific vendors, vendor tags, product categories, or analytical accounts can trigger dedicated approval paths — ensuring strategic and high-risk supplier relationships receive heightened scrutiny.

  • Vendor & vendor tag conditions
  • Product category routing
  • Analytic account & department filters
👤

HR Manager Auto-Routing

Enable dynamic approver resolution by linking approval tiers to the purchase requester's HR manager hierarchy. When a PO is submitted, the system automatically identifies the correct manager from the employee chart without manual configuration per user.

  • Dynamic HR hierarchy resolution
  • Fallback to static approver groups
  • Works with Odoo HR & Employees app
⏱️

SLA & Deadline Tracking

Set response deadlines per approval tier (in hours or business days). The system tracks elapsed time in real-time and triggers configurable escalation notifications before and at deadline. Overdue approvals are highlighted in the dashboard and trigger supervisor alerts.

  • Per-tier SLA configuration (hours/days)
  • Business hours calendar integration
  • Pre-deadline & overdue escalation email
📊

Approval Analytics Dashboard

A dedicated analytics dashboard surfaces approval KPIs across the organization: average approval time per tier, approval rate by department, bottleneck identification, approver workload distribution, and trend analysis over configurable date ranges.

  • Avg. approval time per tier & approver
  • Bottleneck identification report
  • Configurable date-range & grouping
📋

Audit Trail Export

Every approval action — approve, reject, delegate, escalate, comment — is recorded with a UTC timestamp, user identity, IP address reference, and attached rationale note. The complete trail is exportable to Excel (XLSX) and PDF directly from the PO form or the analytics dashboard.

  • Immutable, timestamped event log
  • Export to XLSX & PDF one-click
  • Attach rationale notes per action
Approval Lifecycle

End-to-End Approval Pipeline

From PO creation to locked order — every step is governed, trackable, and enforceable.

1
📝

PO Created & Submitted

Requester completes the purchase order form and clicks "Submit for Approval." System validates mandatory fields and locks the PO from further editing.

2
🔔

Tier 1 Approver Notified

Approval matrix engine evaluates amount, vendor, category, and department conditions. The matching Tier 1 approver receives an in-app notification and email with SLA deadline.

3
🔁

Sequential Escalation

Upon Tier 1 approval, the system automatically advances to Tier 2 (and beyond). If a deadline is missed, the workflow escalates to the supervisor and flags the SLA breach on the dashboard.

4
🔒

Final Approval & Lock

All required tiers approved. The PO status advances to "Approved," the order is confirmed and locked. The complete audit trail is sealed and available for export or attachment to the PO record.

⚠
Rejection Path: Any tier approver may reject the PO with a mandatory reason. The requester is immediately notified, the PO is returned to draft for revision or cancellation, and the rejection event is permanently recorded in the audit trail.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common technical and functional questions from procurement teams.

Q

Can I configure different approval matrices for different companies in a multi-company Odoo setup?

Yes. Each approval matrix record is scoped to a specific company. In a multi-company environment, when a PO is submitted, the system evaluates only the active matrices belonging to the company of that PO. This means Company A can require three approval tiers for orders above $10,000 while Company B uses a single approver for all purchases — all within the same Odoo instance. Matrix configuration is accessible only to administrators of the respective company.

Q

What happens to in-progress PO approvals if a user who is designated as an approver leaves the company or their account is archived?

The module includes an automatic fallback mechanism. When an approver's account is archived or deactivated, any POs pending their approval are automatically escalated to the configured escalation target for that tier (typically the tier's backup approver group or supervisor). An administrator notification is also triggered so the matrix configuration can be updated with the replacement approver. Existing completed approval records (audit log entries) are never deleted and always retain the original approver's name for historical accuracy.

Q

Does the module support parallel (simultaneous) approval at a given tier, or is it strictly sequential?

Both modes are supported and configurable per tier. In Sequential Mode, approvers within a tier are notified one at a time in a defined order — the next approver only receives the request after the preceding one has approved. In Parallel Mode, all approvers in the tier are notified simultaneously, and the tier advances as soon as the required number (configurable: any one, a majority, or all) approve. You can mix modes across tiers — for example, Tier 1 can be parallel (any one of three managers approves) while Tier 2 is sequential (CFO must approve after Procurement Director).
