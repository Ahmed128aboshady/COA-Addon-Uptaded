Core Features

Six Powerful Guarantee Management Pillars

Each module pillar is engineered to handle a specific guarantee type or cross-cutting concern, giving your team a comprehensive, enterprise-ready solution.

🏰

Bid Bonds

Issue and track bid bonds (tender guarantees) issued on behalf of your company to support tender submissions. Full lifecycle from draft to release or forfeiture upon tender award or rejection.

  • Link to tender/project reference
  • Beneficiary (government/client) tracking
  • Automatic release upon tender outcome
  • Commission and bank fee accounting
📚

Performance / Final Bonds

Manage performance bonds (final bonds) issued to guarantee successful contract execution. Typically 5–10% of contract value, valid for the contract duration. Supports partial forfeiture scenarios.

  • Contract value & percentage tracking
  • Multi-stage release milestones
  • Partial forfeit with residual tracking
  • Linked to Odoo sale/purchase orders
💰

Advance Payment Guarantees

Issue guarantees covering advance payments received from clients. Automatically reduces the guarantee value as work progresses and deductions are applied against invoices.

  • Advance amount & deduction schedule
  • Progressive value reduction tracking
  • Invoice deduction reconciliation
  • Currency conversion for multi-FX contracts
🔧

Maintenance Bonds

Track maintenance/warranty bonds issued after project completion to cover the defects liability period. Typically 1–2 years post-handover. Automatic expiry management with renewal prompts.

  • Defects liability period management
  • Handover date tracking
  • Renewal reminder automation
  • Clean release process post-warranty
📈

Automated Double-Entry Accounting

Every guarantee lifecycle event generates the correct debit and credit journal entries per your chart of accounts — eliminating manual posting errors and ensuring financial statement accuracy at all times.

  • Configurable account mapping per LG type
  • Commission & bank fee auto-entries
  • Release and forfeit reversal entries
  • Full reconciliation with bank statements
🌎

Multi-Bank & Multi-Currency

Support operations across multiple banking relationships and currencies. Each guarantee is tied to a specific issuing bank and can be denominated in any currency supported by Odoo with automatic exchange rate application.

  • Multiple bank partner management
  • Per-guarantee currency denomination
  • Automatic currency revaluation
  • Bank-level reporting and analysis
Guarantee Lifecycle

4-Stage Structured Pipeline

Every letter of guarantee follows a structured, auditable workflow with automatic accounting entries generated at each transition.

1
Draft Registration
Record all guarantee details — beneficiary, issuing bank, amount, currency, validity dates, and linked documents.
Accounting Entry
Dr Off-Balance Memo (LG Value)
Cr Off-Balance Counter (LG Value)
2
Bank Issuance & Confirmation
Bank issues the guarantee letter. Commission paid and contingent liability recognized. Guarantee becomes legally active.
Accounting Entry
Dr Bank Commission Expense
Cr Bank / Cash Account
Dr LG Contingent Asset
Cr LG Contingent Liability
3
Active Monitoring
Guarantee is live. System monitors expiry dates, sends renewal alerts, and tracks any amendments (extensions, value changes).
Accounting Entry (Extension)
Dr Commission Expense (Extension Fee)
Cr Bank / Payable Account
4
Release or Forfeit
Guarantee is either returned by the beneficiary (release) or claimed against the company (forfeit). Corresponding entries close the liability.
Accounting Entry (Release)
Dr LG Contingent Liability
Cr LG Contingent Asset
Cr Off-Balance Memo (Reversal)
On Forfeit (Claim):
Dr LG Contingent Liability | Cr Bank Account

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q
Does this module support all four types of letters of guarantee, or only specific ones?
Yes — the module supports all four major types used in commercial and government contracting: Bid Bonds (tender guarantees), Performance Bonds (final bonds), Advance Payment Guarantees, and Maintenance/Warranty Bonds. Each type has its own dedicated list and form views, tailored fields, and appropriate accounting entries. You can use one, some, or all four types depending on your business needs.
Q
How are the accounting entries generated — do I need to manually create journal entries for each guarantee?
No manual journal entries are required. The module generates all accounting entries automatically at each lifecycle transition. When a guarantee is confirmed (issued), extended, released, or forfeited, the system creates the appropriate debit and credit moves in Odoo Accounting using the account mappings you configure in the module settings. This eliminates human error and ensures consistency across all guarantee transactions. You can review, filter, and reconcile all generated entries directly from the guarantee form using the smart button.
Q
Can this module work with multiple banks and multiple currencies simultaneously?
Absolutely. The module is designed for multi-bank, multi-currency environments from the ground up. Each letter of guarantee can be assigned to a specific issuing bank partner (from Odoo's partner model) and denominated in any currency that is active in your Odoo instance. The module leverages Odoo's built-in multi-currency engine for exchange rate conversions, meaning that FX revaluation at reporting dates is handled by standard Odoo mechanisms without any additional configuration. This makes the module suitable for companies operating across different countries or conducting international contracting.
