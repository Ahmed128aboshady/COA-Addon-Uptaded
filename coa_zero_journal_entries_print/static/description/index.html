In-Depth Functional Capabilities & Key Features

1. Comprehensive Financial & Accounting Control

  • Streamlines general ledger entries, journal validations, and reconciliation.
  • Automates multi-currency journal adjustments and exchange balance tracking.
  • Seamless integration with Odoo Invoicing, Accounting, and Bank Reconciliation.

2. Automated Payment & Cheque Management

  • Tracks PDC (Post Dated Cheques), letters of guarantee, and bank vouchers.
  • Clear visibility on vendor bills, customer deposits, and settlement status.
  • Includes automated notifications for due dates and bank clearing status.

3. Restrict Access & Audit Log Security

  • Restricts sensitive financial journals and account balances by user group.
  • Maintains a complete digital audit trail of all entry postings and reverses.
  • Ensures strict compliance with accounting controls and auditing standards.

4. Custom Financial Vouchers & PDF Reporting

  • Prints Arabic & English PDF financial vouchers and partner statements.
  • Optimized for fast generation with zero impact on database performance.
  • Backed by certified financial accounting experts at COA.
